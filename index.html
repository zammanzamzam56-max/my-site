What Problem Salecompass Targets?

Marketing is complex. Customers jump between Instagram, Google, websites, email, delivery apps, and physical stores. SMEs don’t have the tools or time to measure everything, so money is often wasted on guesswork.

Most businesses don’t know

  • Which channel brings the highest ROI
  • What actually influences buying decisions
  • How many touchpoints lead to a sale
  • Why campaigns work or fail
  • Which customer segments are changing trends

The Salecompass Solution

One AI-powered platform that unifies all marketing and sales data—digital and physical—into a live dashboard.

  • Track customer journeys across all channels
  • See which campaigns drive real sales
  • Predict preferences and menu/product trends
  • Optimise spend in real time
  • Measure offline + online behaviour together
