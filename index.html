What Problem Does SaleCompass Solve?

Marketing is complex. Customers jump between Instagram, Google, websites, email, delivery apps, and physical stores. SMEs don’t have the tools or time to measure everything — so money is wasted on guesswork.

  • Which channel brings the highest ROI
  • What actually influences buying decisions
  • How many touchpoints lead to a sale
  • Why campaigns work or fail
  • Which customer segments are changing trends

The SaleCompass Solution

SaleCompass unifies all marketing and sales data — digital and physical — into a single, real-time AI-powered dashboard.

  • Track customer journeys across all channels
  • See which campaigns drive actual sales
  • Predict preferences and menu trends
  • Optimise spend with AI recommendations
  • Measure offline + online behaviour together

Multi-Channel Attribution Engine

Know the true value of every channel across Instagram, Google, TikTok, email, SMS, and in-store touchpoints.

Predictive Analytics
