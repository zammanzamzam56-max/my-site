About SaleCompass

A modern AI-powered marketing intelligence platform built to help SMEs understand what truly drives results across online and offline touchpoints.

What is SaleCompass?

SaleCompass is an AI-driven marketing intelligence platform designed for small and medium sized businesses. In today’s world, customers move between Instagram, Google, TikTok, delivery apps, websites, email, and physical stores. Traditional analytics fail to explain what actually works.

SaleCompass solves that problem by unifying digital and offline data into a single, real-time intelligence system. Through predictive analytics, sentiment analysis, multi-touch attribution, and POS integration, SaleCompass helps businesses make smarter, data-backed decisions.

Our Mission

To empower small and medium-sized businesses with intelligent, data-driven marketing decisions by unifying online and offline customer insights. SaleCompass uses advanced AI, machine learning, and attribution modelling to help brands invest in the right channels, optimise campaigns in real time, and create meaningful customer experiences that lead to measurable growth.

Our Vision

To become the UK’s most trusted AI-powered marketing intelligence platform enabling every business, regardless of size, to understand its customers, forecast demand, and make smarter, faster, and more profitable marketing decisions.
