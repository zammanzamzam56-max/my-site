About SaleCompass

A modern AI-powered marketing intelligence platform helping SMEs understand what truly drives results across online and offline touchpoints.

What is SaleCompass?

SaleCompass is an AI-driven marketing intelligence platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses. Customers today move between Instagram, Google, TikTok, delivery apps, email, websites and physical stores — making it nearly impossible to understand what actually works.

SaleCompass solves this by unifying all digital + offline data into one real-time intelligence system. Through predictive analytics, sentiment analysis, multi-touch attribution and POS integration, SaleCompass helps businesses make smarter, data-driven decisions.

Our Mission

To empower SMEs with intelligent, data-driven marketing decisions by unifying online and offline customer insights. Through advanced AI, machine learning, and attribution modelling, SaleCompass helps businesses invest in the right channels, optimise in real-time, and build meaningful customer experiences that drive measurable growth.

Our Vision

To become the UK’s most trusted AI-powered marketing intelligence platform — enabling every business, regardless of size, to understand customers, forecast demand, and make smarter, more profitable decisions.
