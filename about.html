About SaleCompass

A modern AI-powered marketing intelligence platform helping SMEs understand what truly drives results across every online and offline touchpoint.

What is SaleCompass?

SaleCompass is an AI-driven marketing intelligence platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses. Customers switch constantly between Instagram, Google, TikTok, email, delivery apps, websites, and physical stores — making it nearly impossible to know what actually works.

SaleCompass unifies all digital + offline data into one intelligent dashboard, using predictive analytics, sentiment analysis, multi-touch attribution, and POS integration to help teams make smarter decisions.
