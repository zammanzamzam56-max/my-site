Research & Development

Built on evidence. Validated by the global research community.

SaleCompass is not built on assumptions or intuition. It is a platform grounded in peer-reviewed research, international academic collaboration, real-world SME interviews, and a consistent body of published work that directly informed its architecture, methodology, design decisions, and every feature and framework you see today.

Jeddah, Saudi Arabia · IEEE · 2026

Presented original research titled "A Systematic Literature Review of Multi-Channel Analytics and Point-of-Sale (POS) Integration in the UK Food Sector" at the IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry, held in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation, with conferences recognised globally as the benchmark for engineering and technology research. The paper proposed an integrated AI-based analytics framework addressing a critical and underserved gap in how UK businesses connect digital marketing activity to real revenue outcomes. My collaboration with Ayesha Anees Zaveri led to the co-authored paper "A Systematic Literature Review of Multi-Channel Analytics and Point-of-Sale (POS) Integration in the UK Food Sector." The research tackled a real and pressing challenge, how UK food businesses integrate data across multiple channels. Drawing on Zaveri's expertise in intelligent systems and AI-driven software, we developed an integrated framework that brings these data streams together, offering a practical and academically grounded solution. The paper was presented and published in the proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry (ICAIII 2026), Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, an internationally recognised, Scopus and Web of Science indexed venue.
