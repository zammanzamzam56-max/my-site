📘 Publication 1: Ethical AI in Marketing

AI in Marketing: Ethical Challenges and Solutions for Consumers and Society

Authors: Danyal Mohiuddin & Dr. Naureen Farhan (Ravensbourne University London)

This study investigates how consumers evaluate the morality of AI-powered marketing and how these judgments influence trust and purchase intention. Using a scenario-based survey of 300 online shoppers, the research assessed rule-based and outcome-based ethical evaluations, ethical judgment, trust, and behavioural intention. Results show that trust is the strongest predictor of customer willingness to engage in AI-driven marketing, and ethical judgment plays a critical mediating role between moral assessment and trust formation. The study concludes that AI marketing must be transparent, fair, and ethically aligned to encourage responsible adoption and higher consumer trust — principles that directly shaped SaleCompass’ commitment to responsible data handling, transparent analytics, and trust-first design.

📗 Publication 2: Multi-Channel Food Marketing

AI and Machine Learning Enhance Multi-Channel Marketing Effectiveness in the Food Industry

This research addresses a persistent challenge in the UK food industry, where businesses invest heavily in multi-channel marketing yet struggle to measure channel effectiveness holistically. The study proposes leveraging AI and machine learning to integrate digital and offline interactions, develop attribution models, track real-time performance, and map customer journeys across touchpoints. It highlights the importance of combining POS data, footfall metrics, order history, and app-based behavioural insights to create a unified understanding of consumer behaviour. These findings directly informed the technical architecture of SaleCompass, contributing to the development of its multi-touch attribution engine, real-time trend detection, sentiment analysis module, cross-platform integrations, and AI-driven recommendation features — forming the scientific foundation of the platform’s marketing analytics capabilities.
