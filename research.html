📘 Publication 1: Ethical AI in Marketing

Authors: Danyal Mohiuddin & Dr. Naureen Farhan

Study on trust, ethics, fairness, and transparency in AI-driven marketing.

This work examines how consumers evaluate algorithmic decisions, how moral judgement shapes trust in AI systems, and how brands can design transparent and fair AI that supports long-term loyalty.
