SECTION 1 — Market Interviews & Need Analysis

Understanding the Real Problems Before Building the Solution

At Salecompass, research began long before a single line of code was written. Our founder, Danyal Mohiuddin, conducted extensive market interviews, discovery meetings, and need-analysis surveys with marketing, food, and retail teams.

  • CEOs of food businesses
  • Restaurant owners & managers
  • Marketing teams & digital agencies
  • Product and growth strategists
  • UK SME founders in retail & hospitality

A recurring pattern emerged: Businesses have data everywhere — but almost no clarity about what drives revenue.

Workshops conducted across:

  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
  • 🇫🇷 France
  • 🇵🇰 Pakistan
  • 🇳🇱 Netherlands

These insights shaped core Salecompass features: attribution, sentiment analysis, trend detection, POS integration, and predictive analytics.
