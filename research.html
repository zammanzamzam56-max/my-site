About the Founder

Danyal Mohiuddin is a researcher whose background in AI, ethics, analytics and marketing science forms the scientific backbone of Salecompass.

His research has been published in IEEE and various international journals, focusing on AI trust, sentiment modelling, attribution science and multi-channel behaviour analytics.
