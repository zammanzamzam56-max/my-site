Built on real market needs. Backed by scientific research. Powered by innovation.

SECTION 1 — Market Interviews & Need Analysis

Understanding the Real Problems Before Building the Solution

At Salecompass, research began long before a single line of code was written. Our founder, Danyal Mohiuddin, conducted extensive market interviews, discovery meetings, and need-analysis surveys with individuals across the marketing, food, and retail ecosystem. This included:

  • CEOs of food businesses
  • Restaurant owners & managers
  • Marketing teams & digital agencies
  • Product teams & growth strategists
  • UK SME founders in the retail & hospitality sector

These conversations validated a recurring problem: Small and medium-sized businesses have data everywhere — but almost no clarity about which marketing channels actually drive results.

To deeply understand customer pain points, Danyal participated in and observed professional workshops across:

  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
  • 🇫🇷 France
  • 🇵🇰 Pakistan
  • 🇳🇱 Netherlands

Each workshop refined the direction of Salecompass and shaped the product’s core features such as multi-touch attribution, sentiment analysis, AI trend detection, POS integration, and predictive analytics.
