Our Core Features

1️⃣ Multi-Channel Attribution Engine

2️⃣ Predictive Analytics (AI Forecasting)

  • Forecast demand, peak hours & trending products.

Outcome: Better planning, less uncertainty & higher revenue.

  • Connect POS, footfall counters & offline promos.

  • Live metrics: ROI, spend, campaigns, trends.

  • Fast decision-making without delays.

Outcome: Immediate action & reduced wasted spend.
